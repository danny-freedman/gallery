My name is Danny. I live in Paris. And I like to take pictures. I started in 2026 after my grandfather passed away and left me his camera, a Panasonic DMC-FZ200. He was an avid photographer. 

Photography is the art of paying attention. By paying attention, by choosing to capture a certain place at a certain time, the photographer can make it beautiful. My grandfather's camera has helped me pay more attention to the world around me and inspired me to make the many moments that fill it beautiful. 

It's also really hard. Photography---composing a frame, getting the right exposure, editing properly yet creatively---requires hours and hours of practice. And so much if it is just being in the right place at the right time. Photographers like Jill Freedman, Joel Meyerowitz, and Martha Cooper have all mastered it. 

I hope to someday capture a frame like theirs.
